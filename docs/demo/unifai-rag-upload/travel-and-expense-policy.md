# Travel and expense policy (demo document)

Fictional content for the UnifAI live RAG demo (WP-200). None of these figures is a real company rule.

## Hotels

- The nightly hotel ceiling is **185 EUR in Paris**, **140 EUR in Lyon** and **120 EUR in every other French city**.
- Abroad, the ceiling is **210 EUR in London** and **170 EUR in Brussels**.
- A stay above the ceiling needs the written approval of the practice lead before booking.

## Meals

- Lunch on a client site is refunded up to **24 EUR**, dinner during a trip up to **38 EUR**.
- Alcohol is never refunded, except at a client dinner approved by the account manager.

## Transport

- Train is the default for any trip under **4 hours 30 minutes**; first class is allowed above **3 hours**.
- A rental car needs the code **ZN-CAR-7** on the booking so the insurance applies.

## Claims

- Expense claims are filed within **30 days** of the trip in the expense tool, with a scanned receipt for any line above
  **15 EUR**.
