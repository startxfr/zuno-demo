# Platform on-call rota handbook (demo document)

Fictional content for the UnifAI live RAG demo (WP-200).

## Rota

- The on-call week starts on **Tuesday at 09:30** and ends the next Tuesday at 09:30.
- Each engineer is on call at most **one week in five**.
- The secondary on-call is the engineer of the previous week.

## Severities

- **SEV-1**: a customer production platform is down. Acknowledge within **10 minutes**, open the bridge "Kestrel".
- **SEV-2**: degraded service with a workaround. Acknowledge within **30 minutes**.
- **SEV-3**: no customer impact. Handle on the next working day.

## Compensation

- Each on-call week pays a flat **420 EUR**, plus **65 EUR** per night with a call-out.
- A call-out after 22:00 gives a late start the next morning (**11:00** at the latest).
