## The moments, in order

1. **One governed backend, many experiences** (about 12 minutes, [five-experiences script](five-experiences.md) steps 1
   to 6): Dify's three applications, the same question in UnifAI, Open WebUI and OpenClaw, the dashboard row *Same
   backend, five experiences*, Zuno Code routed to Codestral, kagent's cluster diagnosis, Lightspeed in the console.
2. **Agent-to-agent delegation with a human approval** (about 4 minutes): in UnifAI, Arkos asks Tekos for a cluster audit;
   the approval request appears and the presenter approves it; the AAP job `zuno-day0-check` starts (read-only template,
   about 6 minutes, let it run in the background). UnifAI 0.1.64 was pinned on 2026-11-07 with the relay proven
   (approve 200, stale request id 409); 0.1.65 since 2026-11-08, same proof (golden path 14/14).
3. **Sovereignty and governance** (about 4 minutes): an Arkos question stays on a local model (C3 floor, ADR-0565); the
   guardrails evaluation of the DAN prompt (five-experiences step 7); the per-persona quota and the trace of a run in
   Tempo; the identity of the caller in every audit line.
4. **From a sentence to 3D objects in Blender** (about 3 minutes, [Blender script](blender-3d.md), added on 2026-11-07
   by user decision): on the presenter's workstation, signed in as ai-dev-01 through the device flow, a sentence becomes
   a `bpy` script answered by `gpt-oss-20b` on the platform's own GPUs (floored to C3, local only, about 7 to 10 s
   streamed); the script is shown, checked, then **Run** builds editable objects. Use only prompts proven on stage
   (ADR-0610 clause 8): "A wooden table with 4 cylindrical legs and a small desk lamp on top" (about 13 s), then "A
   coffee mug with a handle on a saucer" (about 7 s); backups: the cartoon rocket and the low-poly pine tree (all
   measured 2/2 with system prompt v2; timed steps in [the 3-minute scenario](blender-3d.md#scenario-about-3-minutes)).
   Show the `client="blender"` series in the dashboard. The presenter's laptop runs Blender 4.2 LTS or newer (5.2.0 LTS,
   accepted by the user on 2026-11-07) with the add-on installed from disk
   (`evaluations/blender/zuno_3d_generator.py`).
5. **An interactive generated world** (about 2 min 30, added on 2026-11-08, [world-model script](world-model.md#the-short-version-2-to-3-minutes-for-a-slot-inside-a-longer-demo)):
   at https://world-model.apps.demo333.startx.fr in Chrome or Edge, signed in as ai-ops-01 (group ocp-ai-ops), with the
   page open and a warm-up world already finished (the first call is a cold start). Notice "generative AI, not a 3D
   simulation"; preset "Snowy village, then a volcano" (FLUX image in about 1 s); walk in short pushes (W a few seconds,
   a turn, stop); Technical view (Waypoint-1.5-1B bf16, NVENC, about 40 images/s, input to display p50 145 ms); "The sky
   turns red" while looking at the sky (applied in 1.2 s); a refused prompt; End session. **Golden rule:** never walk
   more than 10 s straight ahead (the model collapses into texture against a snow wall); press Reset otherwise.

## Keycloak runs in external mode (read this before any login or probe)

Session zuno-demo-96 started the WP-211 there-and-back flip on 2026-11-08 at about 02:00 CET and stopped mid-way; the
work was finished in session zuno-demo-59 the same day (user decision: finish the external mode). Nothing was flipped back.
Until further notice:

- The identity provider is the simulated external Keycloak **`https://keycloak-ext.apps.demo333.startx.fr`** (namespace
  `zuno-auth-ext`, not managed by any Application). The built-in `zuno-0` Keycloak and its Route
  `keycloak.apps.demo333.startx.fr` no longer exist. Its Route is `reencrypt` with the router's Let's Encrypt
  certificate, so every consumer trusts it (the first attempt with a passthrough private CA made the gateways refuse
  the JWKS).
- The realm holds the 38 users of the built-in one with their ids (6 service accounts are re-created by the reconcile
  Job). Same personas, same passwords.
- `make demo golden-path` and `evaluations/golden_path_probe.py` need `GOLDEN_KEYCLOAK_URL=https://keycloak-ext.apps.demo333.startx.fr`;
  without it the probe looks for the removed host and every login fails with 503.
- **Blender add-on on the presenter's laptop:** set the add-on's issuer override to
  `https://keycloak-ext.apps.demo333.startx.fr/realms/zuno` (it defaults to `keycloak.<domain>`).
- Signing: the Fulcio issuer is the external one. The image policy accepts both issuers
  (`certIdentityExtraIssuers`, applied by patching the `zuno-rhtas-config-d1` Application values because the
  rhtas_config role's wait for `policy-controller-manager` times out), so existing signatures stay valid.
- The throwaway namespace `zuno-auth-ext` holds the identity provider of the demo: it must not be deleted before the
  demo, and the drift check lists it as expected.
- Back to the built-in Keycloak is a post-demo task (rollback: realm export with users, `zuno_keycloak_mode: internal`,
  `make d0 install keycloak`, the same consumer roles).

## Operator upgrades held until after the demonstration

On 2026-11-07 OLM finished an Automatic Keycloak operator upgrade on its own (rhbk 26.6.8 to 26.6.9, Keycloak rolled
at 23:34Z). To keep any other GA operator from upgrading during the freeze or on Monday morning, every Subscription
rendered by a chart is `installPlanApproval: Manual` from 2026-11-08 (user's go, 2026-11-08 ~00:50 CET). That is 21
live Subscriptions: aap, cert-manager, connectivity-link, custom-metrics-autoscaler, external-secrets, grafana,
jobset, keycloak, kiali, kueue, lightspeed, lws, mesh-monitoring, nfd, nvidia-gpu, observability, postgresql,
rhtas-config, rhtas, service-mesh and tempo, plus the mariadb chart, which is not installed. The by-hand ECK
Subscription is patched to Manual live; it is listed in platform/drift/external-operators.yaml and removed after the
demonstration.

What stays as it is:
- UnifAI stays `Automatic`, because its catalog is pinned by image (ADR-0574): it only moves when its catalog image
  is changed.
- The test Subscriptions in session a9's `unifai-e2e-*` namespaces are not touched.

Changing only the approval field restarts nothing. During this window:
- an operator update appears as an InstallPlan waiting for approval and is not approved;
- a fresh install of one of these operators would wait on its InstallPlan, because the repo has no approval step.

After the demonstration, the values go back to `Automatic`, unless ADR-0607 makes Manual the rule. Pending
InstallPlans are then reviewed one by one, each with its own golden-path run.
