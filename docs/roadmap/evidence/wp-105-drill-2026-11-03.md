# WP-105: the GPU failover drill replayed with the Tekos control (2026-11-03)

`make d3 scenario-failover-node`, run by the user in an interactive terminal on demo333 with the agents release v0.9.2 admitted, after the GPU nodes and the embedding model came back.
The drill cordons `ip-10-18-18-19` (it carries `qwen35-9b-wesh`), deletes the wesh pod, probes Comage and Tekos, then uncordons on the user's confirmation.
The verdict JSON printed in the user's terminal was not captured here: the figures below come from Tempo (spans `model_call`, read from the ai-gateway pod) and from the cluster.
Peers (da, 40) were told before the start and kept their gateway tests and builds out of the window.

## What the spans show (UTC, agents `comage` and `tekos`, classification C2)

| Time | Phase | Agent | Provider | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01:37:18 | baseline | comage | `local-wesh-maas` | success |
| 01:37:24 | baseline | tekos | `local-gpt-oss-maas` | success |
| 01:38:35, 01:38:40 | failover (wesh pod deleted, node cordoned) | comage | `local-qwen35-maas` | success |
| 01:38:59 | failover | tekos | `local-gpt-oss-maas` | success |
| 01:46:05 | restore (node uncordoned, wesh pod rescheduled) | comage | `local-wesh-maas` | success |
| 01:46:10 | restore | tekos | `local-gpt-oss-maas` | success |

In the 01:35 to 01:50 window there are seven spans for these two agents and none names an external provider. Comage goes `local-wesh-maas` to `local-qwen35-maas` and back, as ADR-0536 expects.
Tekos is answered locally in all three phases (`answered-local` of the control): a local model was always available, so the refusal branch of the control (503, never an external call) was not exercised by this run.

## After the drill

`ip-10-18-18-19` is `Ready` with no cordon, `qwen35-9b-wesh` has a new pod `Running` and its LLMInferenceService is `Ready`; the three other models and `embeddings-predictor` were never interrupted.

## Not proved

- The refusal branch (no local model ready for a local-only task): proved on 2026-11-02 with the models down (see the WP-187 exposure evidence, three 503 in under 0.1 s and no `model_call` span), not by this drill.
- A turn on a partly available pool where Tekos has no ready local model: Tekos's preferred models run on the other node, so cordoning this node does not take them away.
- Other comage spans between 01:03 and 01:12 show a chain of local errors (wesh, qwen35, `local-maas`, then `cancelled` on gpt-oss). They precede this drill and were produced by another session's test; they are not analysed here.
