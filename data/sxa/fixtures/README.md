# SXA Demo Fixtures

Only anonymized or synthetic approved demo data may be committed here
(ADR-0025).

`seed.sql` loads a small, obviously fictional sales scenario (5 companies, 5
contacts, a handful of opportunities/quotes/orders/invoices and their line
items) on top of `data/sxa/schema/001_init.sql`. Every name, company and
amount is invented for this demo — none of it represents a real customer,
contact or deal.
